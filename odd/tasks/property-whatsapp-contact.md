# Property WhatsApp contact
Objective: let an owning agency configure a property WhatsApp number/message and let visitors open a prepared chat with that property's canonical link, without an internal scheduler or paid API.
Branch: feat/google-aerial-zones-demo. Route: inline implementation, then independent verification for Risk items 2 (new scoped contact permission) and 4 (concurrent JSON settings writes). Runner: npm test. Delivery: ask-on-risk; no commit/push/publication authorized while chain strategy remains unresolved.

## Specs
S1. “es qe el agendar conviene qe sea directam por wsp asi lo manejamos eso nosotros”: coordinate visits directly in WhatsApp, not an internal appointment scheduler.
S2. “debeiramos agregar para dejar el numero en las propiedades y qe te mande a wsp a un numero de wsp”: store an optional international-format number per real property; an owner-agency OWNER/ADMIN or global app administrator may change only that property's contact. Other memberships, anonymous users and another agency cannot change it. Existing non-contact editing restrictions remain unchanged pending the broader role decision.
S3. “y que directamente te mande en link y que queres conversar osae un msg qe lo elige la inmobiliaria”: the agency controls the prepared message; the button opens the selected number with the property title/link included. It does not silently send a message. Assumption: canonical public URL uses the current browser origin and published-property route, never submitted host data.
S4. Preserve existing settings and other property contacts under concurrent contact/general-presentation saves, preserve legacy configs without contact fields, and expose only the published selected property's owning-agency contact in public details. No live database, WhatsApp account, paid hosting, credential inspection or external mutation is authorized.
